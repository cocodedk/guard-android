First version. Blocks dangerous sites and ads in every app with the AdGuard DNS filter, and refuses connections to known dangerous addresses. A dashboard shows how much was blocked, the state of each list and which apps were blocked; it stays on the phone. Up to 20 notifications each time protection starts name the app that tried to reach a dangerous address, when Android can say. Good to know and an About page explain what the app does. A notice when protection stops. Made for TalkBack.
