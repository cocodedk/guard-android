Privacy policy

Cocode Guard for Android collects no personal data.

Effective 5 October 2026

In short

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Your DNS lookups go only to the DNS server the app uses, and by default that is the current network's own.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Cocode (Babak Bandpey), bb@cocode.dk. Because the app collects no personal data, we hold no information about you in practice. You can send questions about the app or this policy to the same address.

What the app collects

Nothing that can be tied to you. Specifically:

  • No account, and so no name, no email address and no phone number.
  • No server run by us. The app sends no data to Cocode.
  • No analytics, tracking or ads, and no third-party tools for them.

DNS lookups

To filter, the app has to see the lookups the phone makes when an app wants to open a site. The lookups are handled inside the phone. If a name is on the block list, the app gives an empty answer. Every other lookup is passed on to the DNS server the app uses.

By default that is the current network's own DNS server, for example your internet provider's or the Wi-Fi network's. Later you will be able to choose another one. Whoever runs the DNS server can see the lookups under their own rules, just as before you installed the app. The app does not send them to Cocode or anyone else.

There is no VPN server. The local tunnel starts and ends inside the app, and the rest of your traffic does not go through the app.

What stays on the phone

The app counts how many lookups it has blocked. The numbers and the app's settings stay on the phone and are not sent anywhere. You delete them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

The block list, AdGuard's DNS filter, comes bundled in the app. The app does not download it from any server. If a later version fetches list updates, this policy will be corrected to say so.

Permissions

VPN

Needed so the app can see DNS lookups. Android asks you before the app starts for the first time. See the section on DNS lookups for what the tunnel does and does not do.

Notifications

Used to tell you if protection stops.

Everything else

The app uses no other permissions. It has no access to your contacts, your location, your files or Android's accessibility features. The app uses Android's ordinary network access, which Android grants automatically, so it can pass lookups on.

Sharing with others

We share no data with anyone, because we have none. The lookups go to the DNS server, as described above.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing to access or delete. Write to bb@cocode.dk if you are in doubt anyway. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no statistics and no JavaScript. The typeface is served from the site itself, so no third party sees your visit.Like any web server, the server the site sits on may keep ordinary access logs.

Changes

If we change the policy, we change the date at the top and say what is new. Older versions can be seen in the project's history on GitHub.

Contact

Cocode (Babak Bandpey), bb@cocode.dk.
